# Registering Your Account

Before using PiCapes, you must register your Minecraft account.

1. Join the [PiCapes Discord server](https://dsc.gg/picapes).
2. Open the `⚡・machine` channel.
3. Run the command:

```text
/register <username>
```

Replace `<username>` with your Minecraft username. 

4. Wait for a confirmation message from the bot.

![Registration Success Message](/assets/screenshots/registration_success.png)

Once you receive the confirmation message, your account is ready to use with PiCapes.
